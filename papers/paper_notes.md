## Paper 2

Title: AI-driven innovations in Alzheimer's disease: Integrating early diagnosis, personalized treatment, and prognostic modelling

Authors: Kale et al.

Year: 2024

### Research Question

How can we integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) into the management of Alzheimer's Disease (AD) through diagnosis, treatment and prognostic modelling?

### Methods

Not mentioned in the version I could access.

### Results

For diagnosis, AI-enhanced models in neuroimaging (CT, MRI, PET) are able to detect early AD biomarkers and cognitive decline patterns, also improving accuracy in cognitive assessments. 

In treatment, AI is able to accelerate drug discovery with virtual screening, guiding therapeutic interventions and monitoring patient progress for dynamic care adjustments. 

For Prognostic modelling, multi-modal data is used to improved accuracy and uncover disease mechnaisms through longitudinal data analysis and risk prediction.

### Limitations

The research article primarily emphasizes the potential benefits and applications of AI without mentioning any technical challenges in implementation. The challenge in integrating AI into AD research is not mentioned detailed enough by not defining the specific processes of its algorithms. Researchers also mention the complexity in applying AI to AD, since it is a complex disease that widely varies among patients, therefore models are to be capable to adapt to this variability. 

### My Thoughts

I really admire how researchers have been developing machine learning into AD research. However, this research paper does not specifically mention the algorithms and methods in how they integrate the AI model into their corresponding methods.
